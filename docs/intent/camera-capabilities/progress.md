## Decisions

- Each capability is an optional field on the camera block (`createCameraBlock`, `repairCamera`), absent when unset, so older bodies stay byte-identical (asserted in the roll and shot-authoring verifies). Per-key roll and focus live on rig keys (F-1).
- Shake: three seeded sines per channel at non-harmonic ratios, weights summing to 1, applied in camera space at sample time; seed defaults to a hash of the shot id when a technique sets it.
- Body mount: placement-based with proportion bone heights (F-2); chest default 0.6 m out, 0.25 m up, looking at the face; back mount looks forward.
- Shot speed: a constant factor per shot, anchored at its first frame; the camera keeps timeline time (F-3).
- Depth of field and motion blur: accumulation of sub-renders, 8 in the preview and 24 per capture, only for a camera with an aperture or a shutter; real optics (aperture = focal / f-stop), focus on the subject when only an aperture is set. The main viewport draws the lens too when looking through the shot camera.
- Roll sign: + turns the camera counter-clockwise (three's +Z), so the horizon falls to the right; the words say so.
- A re-applied technique stack clears the capabilities its previous stack set (`technique.capabilities`), keeps hand-set ones; a capability change counts in the technique signature only when present (older signatures hold).
- The prompt states a technique's capability in the TECHNIQUE line; the RIG line carries only what no technique said.
- Oversized files (App.jsx, stage-canvas-rigs.jsx, dualview.jsx, shot-authoring.js, camera-move.js, studio-agent-protocol.js, mcp/tool-handlers.mjs, timeline.jsx) were edited line-neutral; new logic lives in new files under 250 lines.
